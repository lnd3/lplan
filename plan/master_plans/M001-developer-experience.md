## Goal

The Developer Experience Excellence master plan establishes a 5-year strategic vision to transform how engineers interact with our platform. We aim to eliminate friction from daily workflows, reduce time-to-productivity for new team members, and create an environment where engineers can focus on building product rather than fighting tools.

This vision encompasses three key areas: **tooling simplification** (making complex systems easy to use), **documentation excellence** (knowledge at the point of need), and **automation** (removing manual toil).

## Scope

**Included:**
- Local development environment setup and iteration
- Internal tooling and CLI improvements
- Developer documentation and knowledge base
- CI/CD pipeline efficiency
- Error visibility and debugging capabilities
- Testing infrastructure and speed

**Not included:**
- End-user product UX (handled by separate vision)
- Infrastructure deployment (separate governance)
- Security policy implementation (compliance-driven)
